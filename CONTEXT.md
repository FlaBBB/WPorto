# Portfolio

This context is the personal portfolio website for Fikri Muhammad Abdillah. It presents a selected professional identity, work, and a path for prospective collaborators or employers to make contact.

## Language

**Portfolio Site**:
The personal website that represents Fikri Muhammad Abdillah through curated professional material and contact paths.
_Avoid_: Personal site, profile website


**Technical Collaborator**:
A peer engineering contributor assessing whether to collaborate through visible technical judgment, craft, and relevant work.
_Avoid_: Recruiter, client

**Technical Profile**:
A curated presentation of Fikri Muhammad Abdillah's skills and technology stack, serving as the Portfolio Site's primary evidence for Technical Collaborators.
_Avoid_: Skills list, stack list

**Source Profiles**:
The GitHub and LinkedIn profiles reconciled to provide evidence for the Technical Profile.
_Avoid_: Reference links, profile links

**Experimental Showcase**:
A Portfolio Site whose visual system and interaction design visibly demonstrate front-end craft, not merely support the content.
_Avoid_: Decorative portfolio, standard template

**Contact Path**:
The direct outbound route from the Portfolio Site to Fikri Muhammad Abdillah's GitHub, LinkedIn, and email.
_Avoid_: Contact form, lead capture

**Visual Impact First**:
A design preference that prioritizes the Experimental Showcase's high-fidelity visual and interactive experience when trade-offs arise.
_Avoid_: Progressive enhancement first

**Reduced-Motion Alternate**:
The restrained experience that replaces or simplifies Experimental Showcase motion when a visitor requests reduced motion.
_Avoid_: Full-motion fallback

**Section Map**:
The opening's navigation: the assembled mark is the control, and activating it scatters the mark into a constellation of destination stars, one per section. It is an overlay on a one-viewport opening rather than a scroll scene.
_Avoid_: Star menu, navigation overlay, hero menu

**Section Page**:
One of the four documents the Portfolio Site serves for its body content — Work, Profile, Archive, and Contact — each at its own route. The opening is a fifth page, and a Destination Star navigates to a Section Page.
_Avoid_: Tab, subpage, section anchor

**Destination Star**:
One section's entry in the Section Map. It is a real link to that section's Section Page, not a canvas hit area, so it stays reachable by keyboard, screen readers, and a missing bundle.
_Avoid_: Nav star, beacon button

**Magnetic Pull**:
The restrained lean of the assembled mark toward a nearby fine pointer. It moves only the mark, never a Destination Star, and only while no link is under the pointer.
_Avoid_: Cursor gravity, cursor tether
